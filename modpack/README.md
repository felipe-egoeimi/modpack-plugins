# Modpack plugin

Modpack runs packs from Claude: tested tools for work Claude can't do alone, such as live lead lists, AI visibility and
SEO audits, site health checks and a whole site as Markdown. Results come back complete in the chat, with files as
links. Anyone can also make a pack from their own setup and publish it.

What the plugin adds:

- **The Modpack connector** (`.mcp.json`, remote HTTP, `https://mcp.modpack.egoeimi.org/mcp`). You sign in to Modpack
  once in your browser; Claude never sees your password.
- **Skills:** `modpack` (when and how to use packs) and `modpack-creator` (how to make, test and publish one; with a
  shell it uses the `modpack` CLI from npm, `npx @modpack.md/cli`).

Spending, new access, publishing and payments are always yours to approve, on Modpack.
